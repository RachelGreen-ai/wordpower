# Raising With Regard Parenting Column Blueprint

Column name: Raising With Regard

Chinese name: 有看见的养育

Tagline: Parenting English for dignity, vitality, and real conversations.

## Why Not "Responsible Parenting"

Responsible Parenting is accurate, but it sounds moralizing. It can make the parent feel judged before the conversation begins.

Raising With Regard is softer and stronger. "Regard" means attention, respect, and consideration. The name carries the column's philosophy:

- See the child clearly.
- Speak about the child with dignity.
- Act responsibly without panic.
- Build an English parenting voice that is warm, precise, and protective.

## Target Reader

Bay Area bilingual parents who often talk with:

- other parents
- teachers
- pediatricians or specialists
- coaches and activity teachers
- family members
- their own children
- themselves

The reader wants better English, but also better thought. They want to talk about children without flattening them into labels, rankings, or anxiety.

## Core Philosophy

This column is not medical advice, diagnosis, or school consulting. It is language and thought training.

Core values:

- dignity over labels
- observation over panic
- vitality over achievement performance
- collaboration over blame
- public school as real community, not a backup identity
- activities as life-building, not resume-building
- bilingual parenting as a complex strength, not an apology

## First Five Lessons

1. Describe a shy child without shrinking him.
2. Observe carefully without rushing to a label.
3. Talk to teachers as partners, not prosecutors.
4. Explain that you are not trying to rush childhood.
5. Choose activities for vitality, not performance.
